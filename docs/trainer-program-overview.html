The whole program

Four lines, from your first week to month 12 and beyond

Girth. Choose interval (2-minute holds, week tables for Levels 1–2) or traditional (5-minute holds that grow by the weeks at the level). Level 1 ends at a gate you earn. Levels 3 and 4 need the month (6, then 12) and sessions that held the time: 30 minutes, then 20.
Length. Optional, beside either girth style or alone. It follows the calendar for three months, then your measurements steer it. Its levels open by the calendar alone, at months 3, 6 and 12.
Feeder and deloads. Feeders join at girth Level 3. A deload week comes after 4 training weeks, then every 3, then every 4 from month 4, counted over both tracks.

The training program, start to finish3
The calendar

A week counts with 2 full sessions of a track, or 3 days

What counts

  • A week runs Monday to Sunday. It counts for a track with 3 days of its plan sessions, or 2 full sessions: sessions that together delivered two sessions' worth of what the plan asked. Two runs on one day are one session; one never counts alone. App's choice: 2 full sessions 0.10
  • 3 days count on the third; 2 full sessions once the track's last scheduled day of the week is done.
  • Feeders, manual runs and your own routines never make a day count. App's choice
  • Each track counts its own weeks. The deload's week counts when both tracks' weeks count, or with 3 days of either that include each track. 0.10

Missed days (interval girth)

  • 1–2 scheduled girth days missed: carry on.
  • 3 or more missed: the week repeats (the next counting week doesn't move you on). App's choice: 3
  • No girth training all week: step back (the next two counting weeks don't move you on).
  • Never a catch-up or a double session. Weeks with a deload, a safety flag or a girth-focus block are never charged.

Breaks

  • A layoff is 7 or more days since your last plan session on any track. The plan steps you back.
  • After a layoff it asks "Were you on a deload?" A reported deload is not a layoff, and it refunds the missed-week charges.
  • The streak follows your training days, not the calendar, and skips deload days. App's choice

How a session is built

Sessions grow from 14 minutes to 50, stage by stage

Work that counts as time at pressure Fatigue block (doesn't count) Warm-up / prep Rest (vented) / tube change Traction (a pull, in pounds) Hand release Feeder

Every strip is drawn to the same scale, from the app's own builder run with default settings. Only the dark-teal stages count as time at pressure: a second counts while the reading is at or above the level's floor (5 hg at Level 1, 8 hg above). Drops, rests, warm-up, the fatigue block, retention and traction never count. The retention hold (5 min at 4 hg or less) is off by default and not drawn.

Girth · interval Level 1 · months 0–3 in the guidance

Level 1: more time at pressure first, then more pressure

Sets follow the table

Your place moves one row per counting week. On a table deload row you repeat the previous sets: the real rest week comes from the deload count (page 16). After row 16, 10 sets repeat until the gate. One set is a 2-minute hold with a 5-second drop, in one unbroken block.

Pressure follows the table, then a rule

While sets are still rising, your pressure follows the table's row (5 → 6 → 7 hg). It only goes up, by 1 hg at most, and only once your last 3 sessions at that pressure delivered their minutes. Once sessions hold 20 minutes, it adds 1 hg per 3 counting weeks up to 8 hg (page 9). Floor 5 hg; cap 6 hg in month 0 if you're new, then 8 hg.

The gate to Level 2

Each of your last 3 sessions holds 20 minutes at pressure, at 8 hg, and both have held for 2 counting weeks. The plan proposes Level 2 and you tap to move, but never on a lighter return day: it comes on the first full day back. You start Level 2 at its first row, at 8 hg. From week 10, yield can add a set (page 15).

Girth · interval Level 2 · months 4–6 in the guidance

Level 2: longer sessions with rests, on to 30 minutes

What changes

The floor rises to 8 hg: only time at 8 hg or more counts. A 3-minute vented rest comes after every 5 sets. A set is added about every 14 days, from 10 sets up to 15 (30 minutes), where the sets stop. The cap is 10 hg. App's choice: rows 27–32 place the guidance's rule

Pressure climbs toward 10 hg

Every Level 2 row holds 20 minutes or more. So from 8 hg the pressure can step up 1 hg every 3 counting weeks, as long as each of your last 3 sessions held 20 minutes, up to the 10 hg cap. Moving up at 27 kPa keeps the count you built at Level 1.

The gate to Level 3

Month 6 and each of your last 3 sessions held 30 minutes at pressure (traditional: its own planned minutes). App's choice: 30 as a condition You carry your sets, pressure and its count into Level 3. Yield (6–12 %, page 15): three below adds a set, kept; three above takes those sets off, but the table's own still come.

Girth · traditional The other girth style

Traditional girth: long holds that grow by the week

The shape

5-minute holds (with a 5-second drop) and a 30-second rest between them. Level 1 starts at 3 holds and adds one every 4 counted weeks at the level, up to 6; Level 2 runs 6; Level 3 the fatigue block, then 6 growing to 7; Level 4, 8. App's choice: 4 counted weeks for the guidance's 28 days 0.10

What moves it

The weeks at the level, as above; yield from Level 2: three below target in a row adds a hold, and it is kept. Pressure follows the interval rule (page 9), except that each of the last 3 sessions must deliver its own planned minutes (15 for 3 holds). From 5 hg that gives steps in weeks 4, 8 and 12, up to 8 hg. App's choice

Gates and switching

Level 1 → 2: your last 3 sessions each held their own planned minutes, at 8 hg, for 2 counting weeks (week 15, month 3, for page 18's trainee on traditional). Levels 3 and 4: the month and those minutes. App's choice Switching style keeps level and pressure and restarts the week at 1.

The training program, start to finish12
Feeder From girth Level 3

Feeders: two short top-ups, four hours apart

What a feeder is

5 × 2-minute holds at 75 % of the day's main girth pressure (a 30 kPa main session gives a 23 kPa feeder), 10 minutes, twice a day. No warm-up, and it isn't scored. It is offered from girth Level 3 if you opted in at setup (on by default).

When

On girth days only, after that day's girth session. The next one is due 4 hours after the girth session ends and 4 hours after the last feeder's start. Six hours is the far edge, not a deadline. 0.10

When not

Paused in a deload week, while a deload is due and while a safety flag is up; off below Level 3. On a gentle-return day it takes 75 % of that day's reduced pressure. A feeder never counts as a training day.

The training program, start to finish13
How the program decides

Each session, the plan asks eight questions in order

  1. Is a safety flag up?"Something's off" before a session, or still numb 40 minutes after a pull: step back on every track, and nothing new is offered. Numbness also means a week off.
  2. A layoff?7+ days without a plan session on any track: step back.
  3. Falling short?After 3+ scored sessions, even the best of the last 3 delivered under 75 % of its target: step back. App's choice
  4. Deload?In a deload week, the count is due, or the target came early (reached in the first 35 % of sets, twice): deload.
  5. Gate met?Propose the next level; you tap to move. Not while a gentle return still runs lighter: the proposal comes on the first full day back. 0.10
  6. Yield off target?Three in a row below: add sets (+1 at L1–2, +2 at L3–4), and they are kept. Three above: nothing at L1; the yield-added sets off at L2 (the table's own still come); 2 sets away at L3–4.
  7. Time to raise?The conditions on page 9, half as often while it is working.
  8. OtherwiseHold, and say what it is waiting for.

Rules under everything

  • No data, no change. The plan never guesses from a missing reading.
  • Nothing blocks. A week that doesn't qualify just doesn't count. The trainer never refuses a START.
  • Every "didn't count" says why.
  • Volume before load.
  • Your edits win. The plan rewrites its own routines (with undo), but a routine you changed is left alone and the change is offered.

Length asks questions 1, 2 and 4 first, then its own list (page 12). A yield change is kept, and the next one needs 3 new readings.

What it measures

Three numbers steer the plan: time at pressure, yield and strain

Time at pressure

Seconds with the reading at or above the level's floor, in the counting stages only (page 5). It drives the milestones (the lowest of your last 3 sessions) and "falling short" (the best of your last 3). 0.10 Girth sets dropped on a both-tracks day are credited when the length session delivered its expansion.

Yield (girth)

After ÷ before − 1, from one session's own pair of readings: 6–12 %, the swelling a session can't avoid included. It steers from Level 1 week 10 (traditional: from Level 2), after 3 in a row. Readings from manual runs, without a same-session baseline, or taken within 4 hours of a length session are left out. 0.10

Strain (length)

One length session's after-reading against its own before, in stretched length: the newest of the last week acts. Target 2–6 % (page 12). The 21-day fatigue figure is shown and moves nothing. App's choice: one reading 0.10

Two ways to measure count equally: standardised (during a 20 kPa, 30-second hold) and at rest. Each is compared only with its own kind. A measurement is due every 5 sessions, or at the 1st and 4th session of a week, counted per track 0.10. Rising at-rest readings while on target slow the calendar steps (page 9). The Tissue response test is the app's own index: off by default, and it never moves the plan.

Rest and return

Deloads come by training weeks, and you come back gently

What comes due. After 4 counting weeks, then every 3, then every 4 from month 4. Girth or length days both count 0.10. Time off never brings it closer. It also comes due when the girth target comes early twice. When the weeks bring it due, the app asks when it starts: From tomorrow, From next Monday or Pick a day; today's session still runs. App's choice: counting trained weeks
The deload week. 7 days from when you start it. Sessions are logged but nothing moves: no counts, no misses charged, and the streak skips it. Feeders pause, and the length track rests entirely 0.10.
A deload you already took. Report 3–21 days that ended within the last 28. It becomes your latest deload, refunds missed-week charges, and brings you back gently.
A paused plan. Resume where I was keeps your level, week and pressure, never higher, and brings you back gently. A pause of 7+ days is still a layoff, so the plan still steps you back. 0.10

Gentle return. Day 1 back runs 4 hg under your pressure, day 2 runs 2 hg under, then full. The work is pulled at 60 % speed instead of 75 %. It applies to girth and the length expansion, never to the pull, and reduced sessions aren't judged. It follows every deload week, every Resume, and any break if you "mark easily". No pressure step lands and no level is proposed while it runs lighter. App's choice: the values

Length Girth Both tracks 0.10

On a day with both tracks, the app only ever takes away

Order

Length first, then girth; or length in the morning and girth in the evening. If girth runs first, the length session says the guidance puts length first, before girth work has swollen the tissue, and offers expansion only.

What comes off

  • With a length cylinder, length ends after its pulls (no swap, no expansion) and girth keeps every set. Without one, girth gives 5 sets to length's expansion, spread across the session, always keeping a hold at its pressure. App's choice
  • No warm-up within 30 min of the other track's session, and girth after length leaves out its fatigue block.
  • The Tissue test runs only in the day's first session.

Spacing

  • After a pull, the next track is offered 40 min later (numbness should clear by then), or sooner on "It cleared".
  • A feeder comes 4 hours after the girth session.
  • Past 90 minutes of sessions in a day, START says so. It's only advice.

Changes apply to that run only: saved routines stay as they are, and the box before START lists every change. Nothing on a both-tracks day raises a pressure. A plan day that reaches 90 minutes gets an "A long training day" card, and Long training days offers: Alternate days, each track 3 days a week · Alternate on my days · Same days, stop growing at 90 min · Same days, no limit. 0.10

A worked example · worked out from the rules

A new trainee's first 36 weeks, as the rules play out

Assumes: new to pumping, 5 hg to start, interval girth only, Mon/Wed/Fri every week, each deload taken when due, every session hitting its target minutes, and no yield readings. Everything else is the plan's defaults. The guidance's own schedule, for comparison, has Level 1 over weeks 1–17 and Level 2 over weeks 18–26 (months 4–6). The example was worked out by driving the app's own engine; its weeks are checked every time the app is built.

What to notice

Pressure follows the table to 7 hg in month 2 and reaches 8 hg in month 3, once sessions hold 20 minutes. Level 2 is proposed on the Monday of week 19 (month 4), the first full day after a return; the pressure stays at 27 kPa, so its count carries and Level 2's first step comes in week 20. The pressure reaches the 10 hg cap in week 24, sessions hold 30 minutes from week 35, and Level 3 is proposed in week 36 (month 8). Each deload is followed by two lighter days: no step and no level lands on them.

Words and open points

The words used here, and what isn't settled yet

hgInches of mercury, the vacuum unit the guidance uses. 1 hg = 3.386 kPa.
Time at pressureSeconds held at or above the level's floor, in counting stages.
Counting weekMonday to Sunday, with 3 training days or 2 full sessions on the track.
MilestoneThe time at pressure a level asks for before pressure rises.
GateThe condition that ends a level: earned at girth Level 1, the month and the time held at Levels 2–3, the calendar for length.
YieldHow much thicker a girth session leaves you, in %.
Strain · fatigueOne length session's change, after against before · the 21-day mean, shown only.
Fatigue blockShort holds before the work at Levels 3–4. It doesn't count as time at pressure.
TractionA pull in a close-fitting cylinder, set in pounds.
ExpansionInterval pumping at the end of a length session.
Deload · layoffA planned rest week · 7+ days without training.
Gentle returnTwo lighter days after a rest: −4 hg, then −2 hg.

Not settled yet

  • Whether a real pump holds a full 2 minutes at or above the floor on every set. If it coasts, sessions read short and the plan moves slower.
  • At Levels 1–2, a run of high yield takes off the yield-added sets, but the table's own additions still come.
  • A step can move the pump 4 kPa: the plan's own figure moves 1 hg, and the pump takes the nearest whole kPa (20 to 24).
  • The Level 1 gate's 2 weeks are whole calendar weeks, and a lighter return day neither holds nor breaks them, so a return week can count.
  • Level 2's rows 27–32 are the app's placement of the guidance's rule. Following them, Level 2 ends in month 8 (week 36), past the guidance's months 4–6.
  • Level 3 starts with the 15 sets carried from Level 2 (30 minutes), above the guidance's Level 3 main work of 20–21 minutes.
  • A level change that keeps the pressure keeps its count, so Level 2's first step comes one week after the move (week 20).
  • Whether the week with a gentle return should count toward the next pressure step. Today it counts; the step itself waits for the return to end.
  • Resume arms the gentle return even after a pause of a day or two.
  • The length load step (0.5 lb) and the traction fit band are the app's own figures.

OpenPump describes what the program does. It adds no training advice of its own.
