OpenPump 0.10 · Measurements · what and how

Two equal ways to measure, each compared with its own kind

A reading is a length, a girth, or both, taken one of two ways: standardised, during the pump's short hold at a set pressure, or at rest, with no pump. Neither is the “better” one. The app simply never mixes them.

Standardised: measured while the pump holds

The vacuum the pump actually reported at the end of the count is saved on the reading. Saved before the count ends or after the two minutes, a reading is not filed as standardised.

At rest: no pump, a ruler or a soft tape

Erect or soft comes from the method itself. In a session the at-rest before and after screens offer the five length methods; MSEG and MSSG are logged from the Log screen. Every reading records its cylinder; stored in cm, shown in cm or inches.

A measured session gives a pair: before and after, from the same session

When the app asks (Settings › Measurements › Ask for measurements)

  • Sessions 1–20 (1 asks every session) · Hours after 10 hours of sessions (1–50) · Week counts the sessions of the track you are starting 0.10 · Never, and you log from Today whenever you like.
  • You can always skip; a skip is not counted as a measurement. Remind me to measure is off by default; turned on, it checks once a day (19:30) and notifies only when a reading is due.

The pair 0.10

What the trainer does with the pairs

Girth yield = (after − before) ÷ before × 100, from one girth session's own pair. Target: 6–12 % at every level, the swelling a session can't avoid included (at Level 1 a floor only). It moves the plan from Level 1 week 10 (traditional girth: from Level 2), and only after 3 sessions in a row on the same side of the target. Since a session's at-rest screens offer only length, a yield pair is in practice standardised at both ends.
Left out: manual runs, sessions without their own baseline, and a girth baseline taken within 4 hours of the start of a length session (“after other work”). 0.10 App's choice: the 4 hours
Length strain from bone-pressed stretched length (BPSSL): the newest length session's after-reading against its own before. Target 2–6 %: over 6 % asks you to measure again, under 2 % for a week asks what the block did. From month 3. App's choice: the window
Stretched against erect over 6 weeks: stretched length up more than 0.1 cm while erect length (BPEL) stays within 0.1 cm: the plan proposes a girth-focus block.
On target and rising at rest over 4 counted weeks: the calendar steps come half as often, with Step up now. 0.10
Tracked readings this week shown against a target of 2. The count is for you; the plan doesn't act on it.
Tissue response test the app's own fill-time index from the same short pull before and after a session; changes under 4 % are normal variation. Off by default for new routines (existing ones keep what they had), turned on per routine: 20 kPa, 60 % speed, 45 s, before and after; a length session runs it after only, never deeper than its work. Skipped in the day's second session, and it never moves the plan. App's choice

Same angle, same framing: photos you can line up over months

The level bubble measures the phone against your saved angle

  • Calibrate once: hold the phone the way you shoot (flat, 45°, upright, anything) and tap Set current angle as the … reference. It is saved at once; Clear the reference angle forgets it.
  • Green means within ±2° of the reference in both tilt and turn. The readout shows the difference (Δ), never the raw angle; before any reference is set it shows the phone's own angle and asks you to set one.
  • The dot moves right when you turn past the reference and down when you tilt past it, and turns green when level. The shutter lights up then, but never blocks you. Tilt and turn at the shutter are saved with the photo.

Line it up, compare like with like, and it all stays on the phone

The ghost

The ghost is the most recent earlier photo of the same view, taken the same way (at rest or held). Its opacity slides, and “change ›” picks another date. Save as default for this view keeps your framing; Match snaps back to it.

Comparing over time

A month calendar shows a dot on each date with a photo of this view; pick two. Three ways to look: Drag (a seam wipes one into the other), Side by side, Blink. Only the same view, taken the same way, is paired; the screen says in one sentence whether the two readings compare and why, with the length and girth change and a rate per 30 days.

Where photos live

  • On this phone only, in the app's own storage, hidden from the gallery. Gallery imports are copied in.
  • The release app has no internet permission (debug builds have it, for a developer's log console), and Android's cloud backup is off for it.
  • They leave only if you choose: Include photos in a PDF export (it asks first), or Settings › Back up now, a zip saved where you pick.
  • The debug log never holds photos or measurements. Erase photos deletes the images and keeps the readings.

2Schema and migrations

  • No schema version for readings or photos. Every key is read with a default when absent (the right-hand columns). Numbers are written as strings because the desktop JSON shim has no optDouble.
  • Old readings: no method → Std; no phase → unknown; no observedKpa → unknown, never copied from holdKpa; no pair → day pairing; no okAt → vacuum kept as stored; cylId rebuilt from cyl on load (Model#linkReadingCylinders).
  • Unknown keys are kept 0.10: top level, readings and photos write back unchanged every key the loader never asked for (JsonKeep, invariant 221).
  • Harness: core/src/test/java/Migrate.java, fixture migrate/main-every-kind.json.

3Ids, pairs and deletes

  • Id = "m" + epoch ms, reserved before the hold (MeasureScreens.beginBaseline) so photos shot during it carry it. An at-rest batch: the first row keeps the id, the rest get id-m<method>, sharing one time and the same photo files.
  • A session after-reading gets pair = Meas.pairFor(session): the session's baseline id, or "-" when it took none. It does not reset the cadence.
  • Delete a before: its after pairs with nothing, never a borrowed figure (Meas.prePostPct). Delete any reading: its photo files go too, unless another reading uses the same path (Gallery.pathInUse).

5Write safety

  • Store.save (app/Store.java) → AtomicFile.save: writes model.json.tmp, fsyncs it (getFD().sync()), then renames over model.json 0.10. The same for asrun.json, tau.json and a restore's staged photos. A power cut loses at most the save in progress. A refused write keeps the old file; a refused rename falls back to a synced direct write (invariant 220).
  • An unreadable model.json is renamed to model.json.unreadable-<time> and the app starts fresh beside it.
  • Photos hit disk at capture, before the reading is saved. Leaving a capture deletes only the files it made that no saved reading uses (PhotoDiscard, invariant 85). At each start, after the load, StrayPhotos deletes reading-<id>-*.jpg files no reading has the id of or refers to, that no open capture is making and that are over 24 h old; never on an empty library or an unreadable store; logs a count only 0.10 (invariant 222).

Hold, vacuum, clocks, the camera pipeline and privacy

6Standardised hold on the pump

See docs/pump-control-guide.md §9 “The measurement hold”, §5 “0.0 means no measurement” and §10 “Stopping and venting”.

  • 9 × 66 2A 2A 00, one Add at 60 % speed with the hold pressure for the count, the same pressure for 1 s, then 66 2A 2C 00. Defaults: 66 2A 2B 99 14 1E 14 01. The pump repeats the cycle.
  • The count runs only while readings are at target (within 5 % or 0.5 kPa, whichever is larger). Then two minutes, counted until Save (HoldWindow.WINDOW_MS = 120 000).
  • Vents at the hold limit (1–15 min, 5 default, monotonic clock), when telemetry is quiet 5 s, when the app is left, or before another app's camera or share sheet opens. Release: STOP, move on at or below 5 kPa.

7The recorded vacuum

  • Taken at the end of the count 0.10 (StdMoment): the tick that opens the two minutes samples observedNowOrNull() → Session.freshBaselineKpa (under 600 ms old, wall clock, device measuring). Save uses it (fileObservedKpa); the value at Save only if it was null; okAt says which. A 0.0 sample means “not measuring”, never a pressure.
  • Photo: inside a served hold's two minutes, the count-end value; otherwise at the shutter. Both by PhotoTruth.shutterKpa, under 5 s old on the hold's monotonic clock (invariants 126, 223).
  • Comparable: both have a hold, same tag, both reported values known, |a − b| ≤ 1.0 kPa on the count-end values (Model.Reading.COMPARABLE_TOLERANCE_KPA, Model.Reading.comparable). holdSec is not compared.

8Clocks and days

  • Reading ts = System.currentTimeMillis() at Save (baseline, after). The Log screen uses the picked date and time, or the hold's start; a future time is refused. A photo's ts is its reading's.
  • Hold limits, the two minutes and photo freshness run on a monotonic clock.
  • “Same day” = PhotoCalendar.dayKey: device-local calendar YYYYMMDD. Windows are rolling, ts ≥ now − N × 86 400 000 (MeasLog.window). CSV times are local, without offset.

12Tilt sensor

  • TYPE_ACCELEROMETER at SENSOR_DELAY_UI; low-pass α = 0.15, the first sample seeds it (Level.ALPHA).
  • pitch = atan2(ay, √(ax² + az²)); roll = atan2(−ax, az). Level = |Δ| ≤ 2° on both axes; bubble = Δ ÷ 15, clamped to ±1; x = Δturn, y = Δtilt (down = tilted further).
  • References in model.json: angPovRestP/R, angPovStdP/R, angSideRestP/R, angSideStdP/R, falling back to the legacy calibPitch/Roll. Old Top photos use the POV slot.

16Privacy boundaries

  • No INTERNET permission in the release build (the debug manifest adds it for the log console); allowBackup="false". The debug log never holds measurements, photos, typed names or photo paths.
  • Out only by the person: Export (CSV; PDF with photos only if ticked) through a one-file content:// link, or the backup zip.
  • FLAG_SECURE = Model.secureWindow, off by default; it is incognito's “recents” switch, not in the master's first set. Blur only changes drawing; app lock stops screens being built.

4Photo files

  • reading-<id>-front|side|top.jpg (Shot.filename) in getExternalFilesDir(Pictures) with a .nomedia marker; scratch file capture-pending.jpg via authority org.openpump.capture.
  • CameraScreen.saveFinal (app/CameraScreen.java): JPEG quality 90, from a bitmap downsampled by powers of two to ≤ 1000 px on the long edge (Photos.decodeOriented). The in-app capture asks the camera for ≥ 1080 px.
  • EXIF: compress writes none; stampUpright adds only Orientation = Normal. No location. Imports are re-encoded the same way, so their EXIF is dropped.

13Align transform

  • Align.T: zoom × fit (0.5–4), rotation clockwise, clamped ±90°, pan in frame px (≤ half a frame). Matrix order: centre → scale(fit·zoom) → rotate → frame centre + pan.
  • Baked into the pixels before saving; ez er ex ey are a record only and never re-applied, so Compare draws the file as is. Per-view default match: 5 % zoom, 2°, 8 px.

14Capture paths

  • In-app camera2 preview (≤ 1920 px) with outline and dial. Any failure falls back to the system camera (ACTION_IMAGE_CAPTURE into the scratch file); a camera app that returns only a thumbnail gets that thumbnail used.
  • Permission off: a card offering the gallery and the app's settings (settings not offered while holding, since leaving vents).
  • Import: ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT image/* (fallback ACTION_PICK), copied, decoded; gal = true, and no tilt, held, skpa or hkpa.

Formulas, tests, exports, code map and open points

9Formulas

  • Pair % = (to − from) ÷ from × 100; none if either ≤ 0 (Meas.pairPct).
  • Strain = the newest comparable BPSSL pair % from a length session that pulled, at most 7 days old (Meas.strainPct); fatigue = the mean over 21 days, shown only.
  • Yield = Δgirth ÷ (after − Δgirth) × 100 from the session record, i.e. (after − before) ÷ before × 100; needs this session's own baseline.
  • Divergence: over 6 × 7 days, cold readings only (not afters), newest − oldest: BPSSL > 0.1 cm and BPEL ≤ 0.1 cm, with two of each (Meas.stretchOutrunningErect).
  • No rounding inside these calculations.

10Windows and exclusions

  • Yield streak (TrainerTab.yieldStreaks), newest first: skips manual runs, other tracks, sessions without a yield, and “after other work” (a baseline < 4 h after a plan length session's start). Stops at yieldSinceMs, so it restarts after each change; 3 in a row acts (Plan.YIELD_DEBOUNCE), from L1 week 10 (traditional: L2).
  • Length: strain > 6 % → measure again, twice → −0.5 lb (floor 5 lb); < 2 % for 7 days (strainMissDays) → a week off if the block reached 2 %, else +1 set.
  • Tissue test skipped when the other track already ran today (SameDay.tissueTestSkipped).

18Known limits

  • The pump coasts in a long hold (rate unmeasured). Since 0.10 the vacuum is taken at the count's end, so drift in the two minutes no longer moves it. Inferred.
  • An import records the live vacuum if one is fresh, though the image was not taken then (CaptureFacts.commitTo passes it).
  • Force-stopped during a hold, nothing is left to vent.

15Exports, backup, restore

  • readings.csv (Export.readingsCsv): ts_iso (local), len_cm and gir_cm (blank when not measured or 0), display pair and unit, hold_kpa, observed_kpa, has_front/side/top, method (STD or code), phase (before / after / blank), kind (standardised / at_rest / no_hold). No id, note, cylinder, holdSec, state, pair or paths.
  • Backup zip: model.json, asrun.json, photos/reading-*.jpg.
  • Restore asks twice. It validates in memory, stages photos in .restore-staging, deletes photos not in the backup, renames the staged ones in, swaps both JSON files by tmp + rename and re-points paths. It replaces everything; nothing is merged.
